# Basic human value networks in Europe, ESS Rounds 9 and 10

Supplementary materials for the extended research project report *Continuity and Cross-National Differences in European Basic Human Value Networks in the Context of the COVID-19 Pandemic — A 20-Country Comparison of ESS Rounds 9 and 10* (DATA72000, MSc Data Science, Social Analytics pathway, student ID 14244386, 2026).

This repository holds the direct source of every number reported in that report, together with everything needed to run the analysis again. The full technical appendix is **[`SAN-14244386-am.docx`](SAN-14244386-am.docx)** in this root folder; it maps each reported figure to the file it came from and lists MD5 checksums for all 105 files.

## What the study did

Mixed graphical models were estimated for the 21 PVQ items in each of the 20 countries that took part in both ESS Round 9 (2018–2019, before the pandemic) and Round 10 (2020–2022, during it), giving 40 networks. The two rounds were compared within each country at the item level with survey-weighted regression, and at the network level with the network comparison test, with exploratory graph analysis as a supplement.

## Reproducing the analysis

Put `02_code/ERPcode.Rmd` and `01_data/ESS9e03_2-ESS10-subset.csv` in the same directory, then put the four RDS files from `03_model_cache` into `multicountry_results/mgm_multicountry_v3/` below that directory, and knit the script.

All `RUN_*` switches are `FALSE` by default, so the script reads the caches and regenerates every table and figure in a few minutes. Setting a switch to `TRUE` recomputes that step from scratch, which takes hours for the 40 networks, the 20 permutation tests, the bootstrap audit and bootEGA.

The analyses were run in R 4.5.3. Required packages: mgm, NetworkComparisonTest, bootnet, EGAnet, qgraph, survey, psych, tidyverse, ggplot2.

The script is in two parts. Part 1 goes from the raw extract to the cached models and the exported CSV files. Part 2 reads only those CSV files and recomputes and prints every number reported in the Results chapter, re-estimating nothing. A reviewer checking the reported figures needs only Part 2.

### Three caches that are not included

Too large to distribute. They affect only the recomputation of the accuracy audit and the community analysis, not the checking of any reported number, because the summaries they produce are present as CSV files in `04_model_output` and `05_tables`. Each can be regenerated by setting the matching `RUN_*` switch to `TRUE`.

## Data source

`01_data/ESS9e03_2-ESS10-subset.csv` is a subset of the European Social Survey Round 9 (edition 3.2) and Round 10 integrated data files, published by ESS ERIC and distributed by Sikt. The subset keeps the 21 PVQ items, the design variables, and the country and round identifiers for the 20 countries analysed here. ESS data are available free of charge for academic use under a Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike licence, and are reproduced here on that basis.

> European Social Survey European Research Infrastructure (ESS ERIC). (2023). *ESS10 integrated file, edition 3.2* [Data set]. Sikt — Norwegian Agency for Shared Services in Education and Research. https://doi.org/10.21338/ess10e03_2

The survey dates in `01_data/ESS_R9_R10_fieldwork_dates.csv` were taken from the official ESS quality reports for the two rounds.
